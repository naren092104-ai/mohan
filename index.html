About the Center

The Material Research Center (MRC) is an advanced research and characterization facility dedicated to high-impact scientific investigations in functional materials. Located in Ramanathapuram, Coimbatore, our center bridges fundamental physical chemistry and engineering applications to develop next-generation clean energy storage solutions, robust solid polymer systems, and scalable nanotechnologies.

Our Mission

To discover, synthesize, and engineer high-performance functional materials that solve critical energy and sustainability challenges through rigorous academic research, state-of-the-art electrochemical diagnostics, and collaborative innovation.

Our Vision

To establish a globally recognized center of excellence in materials science and battery technology, spearheading the transition toward safe, all-solid-state, non-flammable energy storage systems for a cleaner future.

Core Research Areas

Solid-State Electrolytes

Designing composite, ceramic, and polymer solid electrolytes offering high ionic conductivity, zero leakage, and dendrite-resistant lithium/sodium interfaces.

Battery Technologies

Developing robust pouch cells, coin cells, and next-generation electrode configurations with high cyclic capacity retention.

Polymers & Composites

Engineering specialized functional polymeric networks, ionic conductive blends, and reinforced dielectric thin films.

Nanomaterials

Controlled synthesis of 0D, 1D, and 2D nanomaterials for high-surface-area catalytic and electrochemical devices.

Supercapacitors

Fabricating high-power asymmetric and hybrid pseudocapacitor architectures for rapid pulsed charge storage.

Fuel Cells

Electrocatalyst optimization, ion exchange membrane testing, and electrode assembly for clean hydrogen conversions.

Key Lab Equipment & Facilities

Electrochemical Impedance Spectroscopy (EIS)

Precision potentiostat/galvanostat impedance analyzers for interface resistance, bulk electrolyte conductivity, and charge transfer kinetics.

Laboratory Ovens & Air Oven

Controlled thermal annealing, sample conditioning, and moisture-controlled curing ovens for uniform sample preparation.

Magnetic & Overhead Stirrers

Precise temperature-controlled magnetic hotplates and multi-speed overhead agitators for homogenous precursor mixing.

Data Monitors & Workstations

Dedicated high-resolution logging terminals and acquisition systems for continuous electrochemical and sensor telemetry.
